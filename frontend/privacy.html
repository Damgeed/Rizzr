Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Zero Data Persistence

Rizzr does not store your audio recordings, transcripts, or generated replies. Audio is processed in memory and permanently deleted immediately after transcription. No conversation history is retained on our servers.

2. API Processing

Audio is sent to OpenAI Whisper API for transcription and ElevenLabs for voice synthesis. These providers process data in transit and do not retain it. All API keys are stored server-side only — never exposed to the frontend.

3. Rate Limiting

Free-tier users are limited to 3 reply generations per day, tracked by IP address. No personal identity is required. We do not collect names, emails, or phone numbers for free usage.

4. Payment Data

Pro subscriptions are processed via Stripe. Rizzr never sees or stores your credit card information. Stripe handles all payment data securely. We only receive confirmation of your subscription status.

5. Preference Storage

Your selected preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

6. Cookies

Rizzr does not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies.

7. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer user data to any third party. Period.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@rizzr.com
