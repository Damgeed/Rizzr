FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which modes are available now?

Finesse (reply) is live. Ghost, Echo and Vibe are in the works — they share the same voice→AI→reply engine, so they'll arrive on top of what already works.

Do I need an account to use Rizzr?

No signup needed. You get 3 free replies per day, then it's a flat $9.99/mo for unlimited use.

Does it work in any language?

Yes — Rizzr transcribes the voice note, reads the tone, and writes a reply in the same language the note was sent in.

How are audio replies made?

By default replies come back as text you can copy or send. Audio replies in your own voice arrive with the Echo mode.

Is my voice data private?

Your voice notes are processed and not sold or shared. You control what's kept in your vault.
