Your words stay yours.

This policy explains what Rizzr processes, what stays on your iPhone, and the choices you control.

What Rizzr is

Rizzr helps you create replies, generate a voice-style response, analyze conversational tone, and run a local exit-timer simulation. You can use the app without creating an account.

Information stored on your device

Saved replies, Playbook activity summaries, voice-style text, preferences, and scheduled exit-timer metadata are stored locally using iOS data protection or the device-only Keychain. You can clear this data in Settings. Recordings are deleted after processing by default unless you change that preference.

AI processing you choose

Before Rizzr sends audio or text for AI processing, the app asks for explicit permission and identifies the processors. If you allow it, only content you choose is sent over HTTPS to the Rizzr API and OpenAI for transcription, reply generation, tone analysis, or voice preview. You can withdraw permission in Settings.

The Rizzr API processes request content in memory, does not write it to application storage or content logs, and disables response caching. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models by default, but may retain API content for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring unless the applicable account is approved for stricter data controls. See OpenAI's API data usage policy.

Service and security data

Rizzr creates a random installation identifier in the device-only Keychain and sends it with API requests to enforce fair-use and abuse limits. It is not an advertising identifier and is not used to build a profile about you. Infrastructure may process standard security metadata such as IP address, request time, and response status to protect the service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Selected content is disclosed only to service providers needed to deliver the feature you request, as described above, or when required to protect users, the service, or comply with law.

Your choices

  • Use Rizzr without an account.
  • Decline or withdraw AI processing permission.
  • Control microphone and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Clear local replies, activity, voice-profile data, and scheduled exit timers in Rizzr Settings.

Children and changes

Rizzr is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the product or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date and, when appropriate, an in-app notice.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Rizzr support. Please do not include private conversation content in a public issue.
